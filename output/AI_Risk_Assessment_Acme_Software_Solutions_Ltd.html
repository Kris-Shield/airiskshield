📋 Section 2 — Detailed Remediation Procedures
1. Establish Corporate AI Usage Policy
URGENT Days 1–7

Objective: Create clear operational rules defining approved AI tools, data restrictions, and employee obligations.

Implementation Steps:
  1. Draft the official Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for Artificial Intelligence.
  2. Categorize tools into Approved (Whitelisted), Conditional, and Prohibited (Blacklisted).
  3. Distribute policy to current employees and require signed acknowledgment.
2. Data Loss Prevention (DLP) & Anonymization Protocol
URGENT Days 1–7

Objective: Prevent unauthorized transmission of customer PII, source code, and API keys to third-party LLM providers.

Implementation Steps:
  1. Issue immediate security bulletin pausing un-sanitized client code and contract uploads.
  2. Enforce Enterprise Team plans with Zero Data Retention (ZDR).
  3. Implement automated prompt scrubbing prior to LLM submission.
3. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Output Verification Procedure
URGENT Days 1–7

Objective: Eliminate legal liability caused by unverified AI hallucinations or errors delivered to clients.

Implementation Steps:
  1. Designate responsible Human Approvers for each department.
  2. Establish pre-release verification checklist (Fact Check, Security Audit).
  3. Require sign-off documentation for customer deliverables.
4. Client Contract AI Disclosure & IP Clause Integration
HIGH Days 8–30

Objective: Ensure legal clarity regarding client IP ownership and disclosure of AI assistance in deliverables.

Implementation Steps:
  1. Review Master Services Agreement (MSA) templates.
  2. Insert standard AI Transparency & IP Warranty clauses.
5. EU AI Act High-Risk Governance Framework (HR & Customer Chatbots)
URGENT Days 31–90

Objective: Achieve full compliance with EU AI Act obligations for HR tools and automated chatbots.

Implementation Steps:
  1. Establish Risk Management System (Article 9) for HR screening tools.
  2. Perform bias testing to prevent discriminatory filtering.
  3. Implement candidate and chatbot transparency disclosures (Article 50).
