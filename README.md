# AI Risk Shield

## Responsible AI Governance Platform

**Version:** 0.1.0 Foundation  
**Status:** Product Foundation Phase

---

## Overview

AI Risk Shield is a responsible AI governance platform designed to help organizations understand, evaluate, and improve the way they use artificial intelligence.

The platform helps companies identify AI-related risks, establish responsible AI practices, and make informed decisions based on transparent methodologies, trusted sources, and human oversight.

AI Risk Shield is built on one fundamental principle:

> Artificial Intelligence should support human decision-making � not replace human responsibility.

---

# Mission

Our mission is to help organizations adopt artificial intelligence safely, responsibly, and effectively.

We provide practical tools that allow companies to:

- understand their AI usage,
- identify potential risks,
- improve internal processes,
- establish responsible AI governance,
- build confidence in AI adoption.

---

# Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted AI governance platform for organizations that want to benefit from artificial intelligence while maintaining transparency, accountability, and compliance.

We believe that responsible AI adoption will become a fundamental business capability.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Human Oversight

AI systems assist people.

They do not replace accountability, professional judgment, or responsible decision-making.

---

## 2. Transparency

Users should understand:

- what data is processed,
- how AI systems are used,
- what recommendations are generated,
- what limitations exist.

---

## 3. Evidence-Based Recommendations

Recommendations should be based on:

- verified sources,
- documented methodologies,
- clearly defined rules,
- professional review.

---

## 4. Privacy by Design

Protection of personal and confidential information is considered from the beginning of every process.

---

## 5. No Artificial Certainty

AI Risk Shield must clearly communicate uncertainty.

If available information is insufficient, the system must state:

> Requires additional human review.

---

# What AI Risk Shield Is

AI Risk Shield is:

- an AI governance assessment platform,
- a risk identification framework,
- a decision-support system,
- a knowledge-based recommendation engine.

---

# What AI Risk Shield Is Not

AI Risk Shield is not:

- a replacement for legal advice,
- a certification authority,
- a guarantee of regulatory compliance,
- an autonomous compliance decision-maker.

---

# Product Philosophy

We do not use fear as a business strategy.

We do not sell anxiety.

We help organizations understand their current situation and improve step by step.

Our goal is not to stop companies from using AI.

Our goal is to help them use AI responsibly.
