# 1. Product Overview

AI Risk Shield is a responsible AI governance assessment platform.

The MVP helps organizations understand their current AI usage, identify potential risks, and receive practical recommendations for improving AI governance.

The system combines:

- structured assessment,
- AIRS methodology,
- rule-based scoring,
- AI-assisted analysis,
- human review.

---

# 2. Product Goal

The goal of the MVP is:

> Enable small and medium organizations to receive a professional AI risk assessment without requiring expensive consulting services.

---

# 3. Target Customers

Primary customers:

- small businesses,
- agencies,
- software companies,
- accounting firms,
- recruitment companies,
- real estate companies,
- professional service organizations.

---

# 4. User Problem

Organizations increasingly use AI tools but often lack:

- internal AI policies,
- employee guidance,
- privacy awareness,
- understanding of AI risks.

Traditional consulting is often:

- expensive,
- slow,
- inaccessible for smaller companies.

---

# 8. Non-Functional Requirements

---

## Security

System should support:

- secure API communication,
- access control,
- data minimization.

---

Generated reports must be reviewed before delivery.

# 9. MVP Limitations

The MVP does NOT provide:

- legal certification,
- compliance guarantee,
- autonomous compliance decisions.
