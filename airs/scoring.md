# AIRS Scoring Methodology

## Artificial Intelligence Risk Standard

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Draft  
**Owner:** AI Risk Shield

---

# 1. Purpose

The AIRS Scoring Methodology defines how AI Risk Shield evaluates organizational AI practices.

The scoring system transforms assessment answers into a structured view of:

- current AI governance maturity,
- potential risk exposure,
- improvement priorities.

The score is not a legal conclusion.

It is a decision-support indicator.

---

# 2. Core Scoring Principles

## Principle 1

## Rules Before AI

The scoring logic is based on predefined assessment rules.

AI may assist with explanation and report generation.

AI does not determine the score independently.

---

## Principle 2

## Explainability

Every score must be traceable to:

- question,
- answer,
- category,
- weighting,
- reasoning.

---

## Principle 3

## Proportional Risk

The same practice may have different importance depending on:

- organization size,
- industry,
- AI usage,
- potential impact.

---

# 3. Overall Score Model

The AIRS score is calculated on a scale:

Higher score means:

- stronger AI governance maturity,
- better responsible AI practices.

Lower score means:

- higher improvement opportunity.

# 6. Risk Impact Levels

Each assessment item receives an impact level.

---

## Critical Impact

Potentially significant consequences.

Examples:

- uncontrolled processing of sensitive information,
- automated decisions without oversight.

---

## High Impact

Important governance weakness.

Examples:

- no internal AI policy,
- confidential data entered into public tools.

---

## Medium Impact

Improvement opportunity.

Examples:

- limited employee training.

---

## Low Impact

Minor improvement area.

---

# 7. Confidence Factor

Assessment results may include confidence level.

Confidence depends on:

- quality of provided information,
- completeness of answers,
- available evidence.

Levels:

Example:

A company states:

"We use AI tools."

Confidence:

Low

Reason:

# 8. Risk Interpretation

The AIRS score is interpreted as maturity.

---

## 0-30

## Initial

Organization has limited AI governance.

Priority:

Establish basic rules.

---

## 31-50

## Developing

AI adoption exists but processes require improvement.

Priority:

Create structure and awareness.

---

## 51-70

## Managed

Basic responsible AI practices exist.

Priority:

Improve consistency.

---

## 71-90

## Advanced

Organization demonstrates mature AI governance.

Priority:

Optimize and monitor.

---

## 91-100

## Trusted

Organization demonstrates strong responsible AI practices.

Priority:

Continuous improvement.

# 10. Human Review Requirement

Before a final report is delivered:

A human review step is required.

The reviewer verifies:

- logical consistency,
- recommendation relevance,
- absence of unsupported claims.
