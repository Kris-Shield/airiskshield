
# AI Risk Shield
# Decision Log

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Phase

---

# Purpose

This document records important product, technical, and strategic decisions.

The goal is to preserve the reasoning behind decisions and prevent loss of context as the project grows.

A decision is not only what we choose.

A decision is why we choose it.

---

# Decision Format

Each decision contains:

- Decision ID
- Date
- Decision
- Reason
- Impact
- Status

---

# DECISION-0001

## Date

2026-08-06

## Decision

AI Risk Shield will be built as a human-centered AI governance system.

## Reason

Artificial intelligence should support human decision-making, not replace human responsibility.

## Impact

All future product decisions must preserve human oversight.

## Status

Accepted

---

# DECISION-0002

## Date

2026-08-06

## Decision

AI Risk Shield will prioritize methodology before automation.

## Reason

A reliable assessment framework is more valuable than a simple AI-generated output.

## Impact

The AIRS methodology becomes the foundation of the product.

Technology will implement the methodology.

## Status

Accepted

---

# DECISION-0003

## Date

2026-08-06

## Decision

Risk evaluation will not be based solely on AI-generated judgment.

## Reason

AI systems can produce unreliable conclusions without structured rules and evidence.

## Impact

The product will use:

- defined assessment criteria,
- scoring rules,
- documented methodology,
- human review.

## Status

Accepted

---

# DECISION-0004

## Date

2026-08-06

## Decision

Every recommendation should be explainable.

## Reason

Trust requires understanding why a recommendation was generated.

## Impact

Reports should include:

- finding,
- reasoning,
- recommended action,
- supporting source.

## Status

Accepted

---

# DECISION-0005

## Date

2026-08-06

## Decision

AI Risk Shield will not use fear-based marketing.

## Reason

The goal is responsible AI adoption, not creating unnecessary anxiety.

## Impact

Communication should focus on:

- improvement,
- education,
- confidence,
- practical solutions.

## Status

Accepted

---

# DECISION-0006

## Date

2026-08-06

## Decision

The AIRS methodology will be treated as the core intellectual property of the product.

## Reason

Technology changes quickly.

A strong methodology creates long-term value.

## Impact

The AIRS framework will be versioned independently:

- AIRS v1.0
- AIRS v1.1
- AIRS v2.0

## Status

Accepted
