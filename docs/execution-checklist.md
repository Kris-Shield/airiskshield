This document converts the AI Risk Shield MVP strategy into an actionable implementation checklist.

The objective is to move from:

```

↓

```

The MVP goal:

---

# 2. Development Principles

AI Risk Shield development follows:

## Responsible Automation

Automation improves efficiency but does not replace responsibility.

---

## Human-in-the-Loop

Important outputs require human verification.

---

## Evidence-Based Results

Assessment conclusions are based on:

* AIRS methodology,
* defined scoring rules,
* trusted knowledge sources.

---

## Minimum Viable Complexity

Build only what creates customer value.

Avoid unnecessary technical complexity before validation.
