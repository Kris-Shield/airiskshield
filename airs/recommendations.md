# AIRS Recommendations Framework

## Artificial Intelligence Risk Standard

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Foundation Draft  
**Owner:** AI Risk Shield

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the recommendation framework used by AI Risk Shield.

The purpose is to transform identified AI risks into practical improvement actions.

Recommendations are designed to help organizations improve their AI practices.

They are not legal instructions or compliance guarantees.

---

# 2. Recommendation Principles

## Practical First

Recommendations should be realistic and achievable.

We do not recommend unnecessary complexity.

---

## Risk-Based

Recommendations should address the areas with the highest potential impact.

---

## Understandable

Recommendations should be written for business owners and managers, not only technical specialists.

---

## Action-Oriented

Every recommendation should answer:

- What should be done?
- Why is it important?
- What is the expected benefit?
- What is the suggested priority?

# REC-001

## Uncontrolled Use of Public AI Tools

### Related Domain

Data Protection & Privacy

Code:

PRIV

---

## Finding

Employees use public AI tools to process company, customer, or confidential information without defined rules.

---

## Risk Explanation

Information submitted to AI services may require additional organizational controls depending on the provider, configuration, and data type.

---

## Business Impact

Potential exposure of:

- confidential information,
- customer data,
- internal documents,
- proprietary knowledge.

---

## Recommended Action

Create an approved AI tool list and define what information may or may not be entered into AI systems.

---

## Priority

High

---

## Expected Outcome

Employees can use AI more safely while reducing unnecessary data exposure.

---

# REC-002

## Missing Human Review Process

### Related Domain

Human Oversight

Code:

HUMAN

---

## Finding

AI-generated outputs are delivered externally without mandatory human verification.

---

## Risk Explanation

AI systems can produce incorrect, incomplete, or misleading outputs.

---

## Business Impact

Potential impact on:

- customer trust,
- service quality,
- business reputation.

---

## Recommended Action

Introduce human review before publishing or delivering AI-generated content.

---

## Priority

High

---

## Expected Outcome

Improved quality control and reduced risk of AI-generated errors.

---

# REC-003

## Missing Internal AI Usage Policy

### Related Domain

AI Governance

Code:

GOV

---

## Finding

The organization does not have documented AI usage rules.

---

## Risk Explanation

Employees may use AI differently, creating inconsistent practices.

---

## Business Impact

Potential:

- security issues,
- privacy concerns,
- unclear responsibility.

---

## Recommended Action

Create a simple Corporate AI Acceptable Use Policy.

The policy should define:

- approved tools,
- prohibited activities,
- data handling rules,
- review expectations.

---

## Priority

Medium

---

## Expected Outcome

Employees understand how to use AI responsibly.

---

# REC-004

## Limited AI Awareness

### Related Domain

AI Literacy

Code:

EDU

---

## Finding

Employees use AI tools without understanding limitations.

---

## Risk Explanation

AI outputs may contain:

- incorrect information,
- hallucinations,
- outdated knowledge.

---

## Business Impact

Possible reduction in:

- quality,
- trust,
- operational reliability.

---

## Recommended Action

Provide basic AI literacy training.

Training should include:

- AI limitations,
- safe prompting,
- verification practices,
- privacy awareness.

---

## Priority

Medium

---

## Expected Outcome

Employees become more confident and responsible AI users.

---

# REC-005

## Unclear Ownership of AI Generated Content

### Related Domain

Intellectual Property

Code:

IP

---

## Finding

The organization creates AI-generated materials without documented ownership considerations.

---

## Risk Explanation

AI-generated content may involve licensing, contractual, or ownership considerations.

---

## Business Impact

Potential disputes related to:

- client deliverables,
- commercial usage,
- originality expectations.

---

## Recommended Action

Document AI usage in content creation processes and review relevant provider terms.

---

## Priority

Medium

---

## Expected Outcome

Clearer expectations around AI-assisted creation.

# 5. Recommendation Priority Model

Recommendations are classified as:

---

## Critical

Immediate attention recommended.

---

## High

Important risk reduction action.

---

## Medium

Improvement recommended.

---

## Low

Optimization opportunity.
